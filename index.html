Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Welcome to Flames. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Flames application (“App”) handles information when you use it.

Flames is a paper-themed game created for entertainment and nostalgia. The App is designed to work offline, and all game calculations and applicable data storage take place locally on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information through the App.

1. Information We Process

Names Entered into the Game

Names entered into Flames are processed locally on your device to calculate game results. These names are not transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties by the App.

Game History

If you save game results or enable automatic history saving, the App stores those records locally on your device. The history is limited to a maximum of 50 records, with the oldest records removed first when the limit is reached.

App Preferences

Settings such as sound effects, haptic feedback, and animations may be saved locally to remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Information

Locally processed information is used only to provide the App's features, including:

  • Calculating and displaying FLAMES game results.
  • Saving and displaying game history.
  • Remembering your preferences.
  • Providing sound effects, haptic feedback, and animations.

We do not use this information for advertising, user profiling, or cross-app tracking.

3. Device Permissions

Flames uses local audio playback and haptic feedback to enhance gameplay. The App does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location for its core functionality.

These features do not require personal information to be transmitted to us.

4. Third-Party Services

Flames does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services as part of its current functionality. The App does not intentionally transmit your entered names, game results, or preferences to external servers.

The platform or operating system you use may independently process technical information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Flames does not require users to provide personal information to play the game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you have concerns about privacy or believe information has been inadvertently disclosed in connection with the App, please contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game history and preferences are stored locally on your device.

You can manage your data by:

  • Deleting individual game records or clearing your history using the available in-app controls.
  • Clearing the App's stored data through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the App, which generally removes its private local data.

Device backups or copies maintained independently by your operating system may persist after uninstallation. We cannot remotely access or delete information stored locally on your device.

7. Data Security

Flames relies on your device's operating system to protect locally stored information. Although we aim to minimize privacy risks by keeping information on your device, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend using a secure screen lock and keeping your device software updated.

8. Entertainment Disclaimer

Flames is intended solely for entertainment, fun, and nostalgia. Its results are generated using a traditional name-cancellation game and are fictional.

The results do not determine or predict actual romantic compatibility, human feelings, interpersonal relationships, marriage outcomes, or future events. They should not be used as a basis for real-life personal or relationship decisions.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.
